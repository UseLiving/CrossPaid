How it works

One small price engine, four moods, and a monitor with opinions. Nothing here is real market data.

  1. Pick the moodCalm, Pump, Dump or Rug. Or leave Auto-shift on and let the market change its mind on its own.
  2. Read the tapeCandles, volume, a moving average and a mood meter that runs from terror to euphoria.
  3. Make a callGuess whether the price is higher or lower five candles from now, and build a streak.
  4. Watch the monitorHe cheers green candles, sweats through red ones and loses it on a rug.

Questions

Is this real market data?

No. Every price is generated by a small random simulation in your browser. It is not connected to any exchange, token or wallet, and nothing you do here is a trade.

How does the mood meter work?

It looks at how the last dozen candles moved. Steady gains push it toward greed, steady losses toward fear, and a crash drops it to terror. Real fear-and-greed indexes also use things like volatility and social activity, so treat this one as a toy.

Is this financial advice? Anything I should know about real charts?

It is not advice, and it is not a trading tool. If you look at real charts, remember that a chart shows the past, not the future, and a calm stretch can end in seconds. Be careful with leverage, never rely on a single indicator, and only risk money you can afford to lose. A sudden drop in a small token can be a rug, so check who holds the supply and whether liquidity is locked.

ProjectShift is a simulation. The prices are made up, nothing here is a trade, there is no token, and it is not financial advice or affiliated with any other project.
