Questions

Does CrossPaid move money?

No. If you connect a wallet, CrossPaid only reads your public address so you can reuse it in a plan. It cannot sign or send anything. Any real split has to be created in the platform's own official tool.

Is CrossPaid affiliated with a platform or a token?

No. There is no CrossPaid token and no affiliation with any launch platform. If someone offers you one, or asks you to send funds to "CrossPaid", treat it as a scam.

Is this financial or tax advice?

No. How you split income, what is taxable, and what is allowed where you live are questions for a qualified professional.

CrossPaid is a planning sandbox. Nothing here is sent onchain. It is not financial advice and is not affiliated with any other project.
